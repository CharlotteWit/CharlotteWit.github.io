Due Date Calculator

Turn the due months of your EU project deliverables into real calendar dates.

Your data stays on your computer. This page does all its work inside your browser. The table you paste is not sent or stored anywhere, and the page uses no tracking.

1Project start date

As stated in your Grant Agreement.

2Paste your deliverables table

  1. In your Grant Agreement (PDF, Word or Excel), select the whole deliverables table with your mouse.
  2. Copy it with Ctrl+C (on a Mac: Cmd+C).
  3. Click in the box below and paste it with Ctrl+V (on a Mac: Cmd+V).

Please copy the text itself: a screenshot or picture of the table will not work. It doesn't matter if it looks messy after pasting.

The table should have these columns, in this order: Deliverable No (e.g. D1.1) · Deliverable Name · Work Package No (e.g. WP3) · Lead Beneficiary · Type · Dissemination level (PU or SEN) · Due Date (e.g. 6, M6 or M06).

3Your deliverables with dates

Rule used: month N ends on the last day of month N, counting the month the project starts as month 1. Example: start 1 January 2026, month 6 → 30 June 2026. Always check against your Grant Agreement.
